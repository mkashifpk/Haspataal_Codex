# Complaint desks — tested partial delivery

The shared `ComplaintIntake` component serves Reception, Admissions, ER and the ward under each server-authorised handbook entry and service view. It records the original words, contact and preferred means; anonymous/suggestion-box complaints have the documented contact exemption. Department, ward, patient and permitted current stay fields use named directories. A stay supplies its actual patient identity. Patient detail reads check the current identity; a returned master must be deliberately selected before submission. Empty directory fields remain absent.

One frozen request/key protects a new complaint or appeal. A lost answer holds the draft and navigation until retry/readback; printing never creates another complaint. Confirmed tracking numbers appear immediately. New complaint clears an older tracking card. The desk offers no editing/deletion of original words or a recorded appeal. Server `can_appeal` and fresh record authority own the action, including final/late refusal recovery.

Native A4 notice and letter printing reads the server payload in the active window, checks document/record identity and version, and checks actor/workspace. It lays out the server's English or RTL Urdu paragraphs without rewriting them, uses the mapped document printer, and provides separate PDF saving. Cancellation/failure preserves the business record.
